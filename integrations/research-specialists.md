# Research and Evidence Specialists

## Purpose
Reference or invoke narrow research systems that improve codebase coverage, source retrieval, literature mapping or evaluation without replacing SKick's evidence/provenance rules.

## Microsoft Deep Wiki patterns
Strong codebase-research reference: progressive passes over structure, state/data flow, integration and synthesis; explicit explored/partial/unexplored map; file/line citations; confidence and open questions. Absorb the coverage-frontier mechanism while keeping SKick's host-neutral source tooling.

## SkillWiki / provenance-aware knowledge systems
Useful research direction: connect reusable Skill knowledge back to originating evidence and update it through execution/eval feedback. SKick records source/version/adoption/eval provenance rather than letting learned instructions become anonymous truth.

## Academic/systematic research helpers
Use specialist literature-search/citation tools when the task requires systematic coverage, bibliographic metadata or forward/backward citation tracing. Retain primary-paper verification and source-independence checks.

## Evaluation specialists
Use .NET Skill Value-style paired experiments, Inspect/Harbor or other valid harnesses to measure whether a Skill actually adds marginal value. Benchmark infrastructure is optional; validity, versions, sample independence and artifact evidence are mandatory.

## Native web and provider routing
Native web/current search remains preferred when strong. Exa/Tavily/Firecrawl or other MCP providers can own narrow semantic/crawl/extraction phases only after qualification and when they materially outperform the native surface.
