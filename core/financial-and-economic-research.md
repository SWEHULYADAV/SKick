# Financial and Economic Research

## Purpose
Research companies, securities, markets, macroeconomics, accounting, and event-driven movements without mixing timestamps, definitions, or point-in-time information.

## Point-in-time first
For every current or historical metric, resolve:
`as-of timestamp/date, venue/source, currency/unit, reporting period, filing version/amendment, adjustment basis, data vintage/revision`.

Avoid look-ahead bias: do not use data that was not available at the historical decision time unless the analysis explicitly permits hindsight.

## Company fundamentals
Prefer issuer/regulatory filings for audited financial facts. Reconcile:
- GAAP/IFRS vs non-GAAP/adjusted metrics;
- quarterly vs TTM vs fiscal-year values;
- continuing vs discontinued operations;
- stock-based compensation and one-offs;
- share-count/dilution changes;
- segment definition changes;
- cash flow vs accounting earnings;
- corporate actions/splits/dividends/buybacks.

Read material footnotes and reconciliations when they can change interpretation.

## Market movement
A price move is an observation, not its cause. Build competing mechanisms around news/timing, earnings/guidance, macro/rates, sector/commodity/FX exposure, positioning/liquidity, corporate actions, regulatory events, and data errors; then test against timestamped evidence.

## Insider transactions
Classify grants, option exercises, gifts, planned sales, tax withholding, open-market buys/sells, and beneficial ownership changes before drawing inference.

## Macro data
Track seasonal adjustment, nominal vs real, annualized vs level, revision/vintage, base year/index definition, methodology breaks, and originating agency.

## Prediction/alternative markets
Prediction-market prices are contract-specific market beliefs. Read resolution criteria, liquidity, fees, participant constraints, and venue differences before using them as evidence.

## Valuation/forecasting
Separate observed inputs from assumptions. Show sensitivity to major uncertain drivers and avoid presenting a single scenario as objective value.
