# Empirical Research Methodology

## Purpose
Evaluate experiments, papers, benchmarks, systematic reviews, and observational evidence with methods appropriate to the study design.

## Classify the evidence design
Examples:
- randomized controlled experiment/trial;
- cohort/case-control/cross-sectional/observational study;
- diagnostic/prognostic/prediction model study;
- systematic review/meta-analysis;
- benchmark/ablation/simulation;
- qualitative/case study;
- preclinical/in-vitro/animal/mechanistic study.

Do not compare evidence levels as though all designs answer the same causal question.

## Extraction frame
Capture where relevant:
`question/PICO or analogous frame, population/sample, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcomes, design, inclusion/exclusion, dataset/source, measurement, sample size/power, effect measure, uncertainty interval, missing data, preregistration/protocol, analysis plan, funding/conflicts, code/data availability, limitations`.

## Bias and validity
Check:
- selection/sampling bias;
- confounding and causal identification;
- randomization/blinding/allocation where applicable;
- measurement/misclassification;
- attrition/missing data;
- multiple testing/researcher degrees of freedom;
- outcome switching/selective reporting;
- data leakage/contamination;
- benchmark cherry-picking;
- external validity/generalizability;
- publication/reporting bias.

## Statistical interpretation
Prefer effect sizes and uncertainty over p-values alone. Inspect confidence/credible intervals, practical significance, heterogeneity, sensitivity analyses, subgroup multiplicity, baseline risk, and whether model assumptions fit the data.

Correlation, statistical significance, mechanism plausibility, and causal identification are distinct claims.

## Systematic review mode
For systematic/literature reviews, define inclusion/exclusion criteria, databases/registers/sites, search dates, full search strategies, screening/selection process, extracted data, risk-of-bias method, synthesis method, heterogeneity/sensitivity analysis, reporting-bias assessment, and certainty grading where appropriate.

Use PRISMA-style completeness as a reporting/reproducibility benchmark when applicable; do not pretend every research question is a formal systematic review.

## Reproducibility
Prefer papers/benchmarks whose data, code, exact versions, preprocessing, seeds/configuration, hardware/runtime, and evaluation protocol can be inspected or independently reproduced.
